
🧬 Introduction

Organic chemistry is the branch of chemistry that studies carbon-containing compounds, including their structures, properties, reactions, and synthesis.

This repository provides organized study notes, reaction mechanisms, chemical equations, examples, and other educational resources to help learners understand organic chemistry step by step.

 🔬 Topics Covered
1. Fundamentals of Organic Chemistry
- Structure and bonding
- Hybridization of carbon
- Functional groups
- IUPAC nomenclature
- Isomerism

 2. Electronic Effects
- Inductive effect
- Resonance effect
- Electromeric effect
- Hyperconjugation
- Acidity and basicity

 3. Reaction Mechanisms
- Substitution reactions
- Addition reactions
- Elimination reactions
- Rearrangement reactions
- Free-radical reactions

 4. Hydrocarbons
- Alkanes
- Alkenes
- Alkynes
- Aromatic hydrocarbons

 5. Functional Groups
- Alcohols and phenols
- Ethers
- Aldehydes and ketones
- Carboxylic acids and their derivatives
- Amines
- Haloalkanes and haloarenes

6. Advanced Topics
- Stereochemistry
- Aromaticity
- Organic synthesis
- Named reactions
- Spectroscopy: IR, NMR, and mass spectrometry
 🎯 Learning Objectives

By using this repository, learners can:

- Understand the basic principles of organic chemistry.
- Identify functional groups and name organic compounds.
- Explain important reaction mechanisms.
- Predict products of common organic reactions.
- Practice organic chemistry problems.
- Build a strong foundation for further study.

 📖 Study Resources

The repository may include:

- `notes/` — Topic-wise study notes

- `mechanisms/` — Step-by-step reaction mechanisms
- `practice/` — Questions and solutions
